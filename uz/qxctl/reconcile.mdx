`qxctl knowledge reconcile ...` grammatikasi ishchi daraxtni muvofiqlashtirishni bitta aniq bog'langan Knowledge Session Coordinator orqali boshqaradi. Har bir o'zgarish barqaror identifikator bilan kuzatiladi va joriy jurnalning aniq digest'iga nisbatan compare-and-swap amali sifatida bajariladi.

## Buyruqlar

```bash Reconcile grammar
qxctl knowledge reconcile compatibility
qxctl knowledge reconcile begin      --operation-id OPID --expected-journal-digest absent|DIGEST ...
qxctl knowledge reconcile status
qxctl knowledge reconcile checkpoint --operation-id OPID --expected-journal-digest DIGEST
qxctl knowledge reconcile close      --operation-id OPID --expected-journal-digest DIGEST
qxctl knowledge reconcile recover    --discover
```

- `compatibility`: qxctl, koordinator va bog'langan engine'lar protokol versiyasi va doirasi bo'yicha kelishganini tekshirish.
- `begin`: barqaror amal identifikatori bilan yangi muvofiqlashtirish kontekstini ochish.
- `status`: joriy jurnalni o'zgartirmasdan tekshirish.
- `checkpoint`: jurnalni compare-and-swap bilan bardoshli tarzda oldinga surish.
- `close`: muvofiqlashtirish kontekstini yakunlash.
- `recover --discover`: oddiy status mahalliy holatni tasdiqlay olmaganda topish orqali tiklash.

## Tiklash qoidalari

<Warning>
  Ikki slotli tiklash — dalillarga asoslangan oldinga yo'naltirilgan ta'mirlash. U hech qachon mos kelmaydigan jurnal yoki noma'lum kritik kengaytmani tashlab yuborishga ruxsat emas. Agar tiklash muvaffaqiyatsiz bo'lsa, majburlash o'rniga masalani yuqoriga yetkazing.
</Warning>

Topish orqali tiklashdan faqat oddiy status mahalliy holatni tasdiqlay olmagandagina foydalaning. Blokerlarni saqlang; faqat tasdiqlangan dalillar o'zgargandan keyingina qayta rejalashtiring.

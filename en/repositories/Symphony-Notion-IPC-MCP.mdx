This companion project connects Notion-facing workflows to Symphony through a native bridge and optional loopback gateway. It includes local IPC surfaces and helpers for user-operated publishing and tunnel setup.

## What to evaluate

- Inspect the native bridge, local gateway and caller-authority contracts.
- Review configuration and generated connection material before use.
- Exercise the repository's bounded tests on an isolated workspace.
- Qualify actual Notion client, OAuth, page publication and tunnel behavior separately.

This is a separate source integration. Its automated tests do not establish end-to-end Notion client acceptance or marketplace approval, and it is not a default binary component of the signed Symphony beta. Use the repository's current installation instructions and supply your own intended account, connection and publication authority.
